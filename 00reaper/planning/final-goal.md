# Final Goal: Modular, Continuously Improving Workflow System

## Vision
Maintain a next-generation development workflow for both 00reaper and 1000xdev that maximizes speed, clarity, and autonomy. The system should:
- Support seamless onboarding and context loading for all agents
- Enable rapid, reliable scanning and analysis of all codebases
- Ensure all documentation, templates, and checklists are modular, up-to-date, and easy to follow
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement, cycle hygiene, and clear operational boundaries

## Ongoing Objectives
1. **Cycle Hygiene & Reset**
   - Archive and reset workflow files at the end of each cycle
   - Keep all progress, enhancements, and lessons learned traceable
2. **Template & Checklist Quality**
   - Maintain and refine templates and checklists for all workflow phases
   - Ensure they reflect current best practices
3. **Self-Improvement & Backlog**
   - Regularly review and prioritize workflow enhancements
   - Encourage actionable feedback and iteration
4. **Onboarding & Reference**
   - Keep onboarding and reference docs clear, concise, and up-to-date
   - Use brain files as the starting point for all agents
5. **Cursor Rule Hygiene**
   - Periodically review and update master cursor rules for clarity and accuracy

## Success Criteria
- The workflow system is modular, clear, and easy to extend
- All agents and the user can rapidly onboard and improve the system
- Continuous improvement is documented and actionable
