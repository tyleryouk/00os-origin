## 1. Problem Statemen
I've noticed inconsistency in the writing style across my brain files. While some documents, like the requirements file in 97-rules-workflow-system-wide-optimization, use a first-person perspective that creates a strong sense of ownership, many other files still use third-person or passive voice. 1. Reduces my sense of agency and ownership over the cognitive architecture

3. Makes documentation feel less direct and actionable
4. Reduces the natural flow between my thought processes and documentation
5. Complicates the transition between planning and implementation roles
